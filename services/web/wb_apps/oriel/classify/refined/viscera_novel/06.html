While these confessions are admissible (Miranda rules do not exclude truly volunteered statements), they underscore how an offender’s *own guilt or unconscious desire to come clean* can lead to self-incrimination. - **False or Involuntary Confessions and Bias:** On the other hand, psychology warns that **unconscious bias in investigators** can induce false confessions from the innocent.
